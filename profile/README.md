## Finite Sample

Projects and research code related to statistics, econometrics, and machine learning. The repositories vary in scope and implementation language; many explore a specific estimator, design choice, or computational method.

📐 **Econometrics and causal inference**: [LATE](https://github.com/finite-sample/late), [late_iv](https://github.com/finite-sample/late_iv), [tworeg](https://github.com/finite-sample/tworeg), [double-hte](https://github.com/finite-sample/double-hte), [smooth-operator](https://github.com/finite-sample/smooth-operator), and [simcheck](https://github.com/finite-sample/simcheck). These repositories study identification, treatment-effect estimation, smoothing, and simulation-based checks.

🎯 **Calibration, scoring, and decision rules**: [calibre](https://github.com/finite-sample/calibre), [streamcal](https://github.com/finite-sample/streamcal), [fairlex](https://github.com/finite-sample/fairlex), [rank-preserving-calibration](https://github.com/finite-sample/rank-preserving-calibration), [score_signs](https://github.com/finite-sample/score_signs), [winference](https://github.com/finite-sample/winference), [optimal-classification-cutoffs](https://github.com/finite-sample/optimal-classification-cutoffs), [optimal_cuts](https://github.com/finite-sample/optimal_cuts), [queue-shift](https://github.com/finite-sample/queue-shift), and [pareto-deployment](https://github.com/finite-sample/pareto-deployment). They address probability calibration, multiclass thresholds, pairwise rankings, fairness constraints, and deployment decisions.

🌲 **Model selection and stable machine learning**: [stable-cart](https://github.com/finite-sample/stable-cart), [robust-cart](https://github.com/finite-sample/robust-cart), [stableboost](https://github.com/finite-sample/stableboost), [bcr](https://github.com/finite-sample/bcr), [stable-gen](https://github.com/finite-sample/stable-gen), [dct](https://github.com/finite-sample/dct), [act](https://github.com/finite-sample/act), [mpsam](https://github.com/finite-sample/mpsam), [sam-lasso](https://github.com/finite-sample/sam-lasso), [treegptq](https://github.com/finite-sample/treegptq), [stagecoachml](https://github.com/finite-sample/stagecoachml), [ensemble-proximity](https://github.com/finite-sample/ensemble-proximity), [pareto-gd](https://github.com/finite-sample/pareto-gd), [beam-fsr](https://github.com/finite-sample/beam-fsr), and [knockoff-triplets](https://github.com/finite-sample/knockoff-triplets). These projects explore variable selection, model averaging, consistency training, compression, and model updates.

🔎 **Matching, joins, dimension reduction, and smoothing**: [preclink](https://github.com/finite-sample/preclink), [setjoin](https://github.com/finite-sample/setjoin), [pyppann](https://github.com/finite-sample/pyppann), [pyppur](https://github.com/finite-sample/pyppur), [incline](https://github.com/finite-sample/incline), [hbw](https://github.com/finite-sample/hbw), and [alsgls](https://github.com/finite-sample/alsgls). These tools address linkage objectives, projection-pursuit reductions, nonparametric smoothing, and structured covariance estimation.

🧪 **Design, measurement, and data collection**: [fewlab](https://github.com/finite-sample/fewlab), [fewest_domains](https://github.com/finite-sample/fewest_domains), [optimal_data_collection](https://github.com/finite-sample/optimal_data_collection), [prop_male](https://github.com/finite-sample/prop_male), [hybrid](https://github.com/finite-sample/hybrid), [lowdimtraining](https://github.com/finite-sample/lowdimtraining), [swaps](https://github.com/finite-sample/swaps), [onlinerake](https://github.com/finite-sample/onlinerake), and [adaptive-eb](https://github.com/finite-sample/adaptive-eb). They examine how sampling, labeling, weighting, training updates, and stopping rules affect precision and evidence value.

💬 **Learning and deliberation**: [guess](https://github.com/finite-sample/guess), [guessing-and-forgetting](https://github.com/finite-sample/guessing-and-forgetting), [measuring-learning](https://github.com/finite-sample/measuring-learning), [dp-steadier-not-closer](https://github.com/finite-sample/dp-steadier-not-closer), and [dp-deliberately](https://github.com/finite-sample/dp-deliberately). These projects measure knowledge and learning, distinguish opinion change from measurement error, and audit deliberative processes.

📚 **Replications, benchmarks, and teaching materials**: [econometric_bench](https://github.com/finite-sample/econometric_bench), [benchmarking-benchmarks](https://github.com/finite-sample/benchmarking-benchmarks), [dann](https://github.com/finite-sample/dann), [total_error](https://github.com/finite-sample/total_error), [bagged_fsr](https://github.com/finite-sample/bagged_fsr), [bagged_mp](https://github.com/finite-sample/bagged_mp), [endorsement](https://github.com/finite-sample/endorsement), [synth-predictors-op-bug](https://github.com/finite-sample/synth-predictors-op-bug), and [ds](https://github.com/finite-sample/ds). These projects preserve runnable examples, reproduce published or proposed calculations, test methods under controlled finite samples, and document software defects.

🤖 **R interfaces**: [rmcp](https://github.com/finite-sample/rmcp) exposes selected R capabilities through an MCP server.
