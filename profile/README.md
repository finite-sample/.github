## Finite Sample

Projects and research code related to statistics, econometrics, and machine learning. The repositories vary in scope and implementation language; many explore a specific estimator, design choice, or computational method.

📐 **Causal inference and econometrics**: [late](https://github.com/finite-sample/late), [late_iv](https://github.com/finite-sample/late_iv), [double-hte](https://github.com/finite-sample/double-hte), [smooth-operator](https://github.com/finite-sample/smooth-operator), [synth-predictors-op-bug](https://github.com/finite-sample/synth-predictors-op-bug), [alsgls](https://github.com/finite-sample/alsgls), [simcheck](https://github.com/finite-sample/simcheck), and [hybrid](https://github.com/finite-sample/hybrid). Complier identification and tests of IV assumptions, heterogeneous effects, smoothing event-study estimates, a bug in the Synth R package, GLS with factor-structured covariance, Monte Carlo checks of estimators, and evidential value from published p-values.

🧪 **Measuring with ML predictions, sampling, and weighting**: [tworeg](https://github.com/finite-sample/tworeg), [total_error](https://github.com/finite-sample/total_error), [swaps](https://github.com/finite-sample/swaps), [fewlab](https://github.com/finite-sample/fewlab), [fewest_domains](https://github.com/finite-sample/fewest_domains), [optimal_data_collection](https://github.com/finite-sample/optimal_data_collection), and [onlinerake](https://github.com/finite-sample/onlinerake). What happens when a model's predictions stand in for measurements, how to spend a small labeling budget to keep downstream estimates honest, and how to allocate and weight samples.

💬 **Measuring political knowledge and learning**: [guess](https://github.com/finite-sample/guess), [guessing-and-forgetting](https://github.com/finite-sample/guessing-and-forgetting), [hidden](https://github.com/finite-sample/hidden), [partisan-gaps](https://github.com/finite-sample/partisan-gaps), [interpretation_gap](https://github.com/finite-sample/interpretation_gap), [unclear_gap](https://github.com/finite-sample/unclear_gap), [waters-of-casablanca](https://github.com/finite-sample/waters-of-casablanca), [misinformation](https://github.com/finite-sample/misinformation), and [measuring-learning](https://github.com/finite-sample/measuring-learning). How much of what surveys record as knowledge is guessing, whether people know more than surveys show, how question format and scoring create partisan knowledge gaps, how to tell misinformation from guessing and how poll design inflates it, and how to measure what people learn.

🗳️ **Measuring opinion and deliberation**: [dp-steadier-not-closer](https://github.com/finite-sample/dp-steadier-not-closer), [stddev](https://github.com/finite-sample/stddev), [dp-deliberately](https://github.com/finite-sample/dp-deliberately), and [endorsement](https://github.com/finite-sample/endorsement). Separating real opinion change from measurement error, auditing deliberative processes, and estimating support for groups from endorsement experiments.

🎯 **Calibration and cutoffs**: [calibre](https://github.com/finite-sample/calibre), [streamcal](https://github.com/finite-sample/streamcal), [fairlex](https://github.com/finite-sample/fairlex), [rank-preserving-calibration](https://github.com/finite-sample/rank-preserving-calibration), and [optimal-classification-cutoffs](https://github.com/finite-sample/optimal-classification-cutoffs). Making predicted probabilities mean what they say (batch, streaming, fair, rank-preserving) and choosing thresholds that maximize a metric.

⚖️ **Evaluating models and benchmarks**: [winference](https://github.com/finite-sample/winference), [score_signs](https://github.com/finite-sample/score_signs), [benchmarking-benchmarks](https://github.com/finite-sample/benchmarking-benchmarks), and [econometric_bench](https://github.com/finite-sample/econometric_bench). Win rates, LLM judges, and what a benchmark has to establish before its numbers mean anything.

🌲 **Stable predictions and training**: [stable-cart](https://github.com/finite-sample/stable-cart), [robust-cart](https://github.com/finite-sample/robust-cart), [stableboost](https://github.com/finite-sample/stableboost), [bcr](https://github.com/finite-sample/bcr), [dct](https://github.com/finite-sample/dct), [act](https://github.com/finite-sample/act), [stable-gen](https://github.com/finite-sample/stable-gen), [mpsam](https://github.com/finite-sample/mpsam), and [lowdimtraining](https://github.com/finite-sample/lowdimtraining). Training trees, boosted models, and neural nets so that predictions change less across resamples and retrains, plus cheaper gradient updates.

🚀 **Updating and deploying models**: [pareto-gd](https://github.com/finite-sample/pareto-gd), [pareto-deployment](https://github.com/finite-sample/pareto-deployment), [queue-shift](https://github.com/finite-sample/queue-shift), [stagecoachml](https://github.com/finite-sample/stagecoachml), and [treegptq](https://github.com/finite-sample/treegptq). Shipping a new model without breaking cases the old one got right, budgeting how much work an update moves, predicting when features arrive in stages, and compressing tree ensembles.

✂️ **Variable selection**: [bagged_fsr](https://github.com/finite-sample/bagged_fsr), [beam-fsr](https://github.com/finite-sample/beam-fsr), [bagged_mp](https://github.com/finite-sample/bagged_mp), and [sam-lasso](https://github.com/finite-sample/sam-lasso). Bagging and beam-averaging forward stepwise and matching pursuit, and sharpness-aware training for the lasso.

🔎 **Joins and nearest neighbors**: [preclink](https://github.com/finite-sample/preclink), [setjoin](https://github.com/finite-sample/setjoin), [pyppann](https://github.com/finite-sample/pyppann), and [dann](https://github.com/finite-sample/dann). Record linkage tuned for precision, joins optimized for the downstream objective, approximate nearest-neighbor search, and discriminant adaptive nearest neighbors.

〰️ **Smoothing, binning, and dimension reduction**: [incline](https://github.com/finite-sample/incline), [hbw](https://github.com/finite-sample/hbw), [optimal_cuts](https://github.com/finite-sample/optimal_cuts), and [pyppur](https://github.com/finite-sample/pyppur). Local trends in noisy series, analytic bandwidth selection, optimal bin edges, and projection-pursuit dimension reduction.

📚 **Teaching and tools**: [ds](https://github.com/finite-sample/ds) and [prop_male](https://github.com/finite-sample/prop_male) are teaching notes; [rmcp](https://github.com/finite-sample/rmcp) exposes R through an MCP server.
